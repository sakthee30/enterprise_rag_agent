WAITROSE RETAIL POLICY DOCUMENT
================================

SECTION 1: PRODUCT RETURN POLICY
Customers may return any product within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt.
Perishable goods cannot be returned once opened. Non-perishable items must be in
original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

SECTION 2: COMPLIANCE GUIDELINES
All store employees must complete mandatory compliance training every 6 months.
Data handling must follow GDPR guidelines. Customer personal data must never be
shared with third parties without explicit consent. CCTV footage is retained for
28 days and can only be accessed by authorized personnel.

SECTION 3: INCIDENT MANAGEMENT PROCEDURE
When a system incident occurs, the on-call engineer must be notified within 15 minutes.
All incidents must be logged in ServiceNow with priority classification P1 to P4.
P1 incidents require immediate escalation to senior management. Resolution time
targets: P1 = 1 hour, P2 = 4 hours, P3 = 8 hours, P4 = 24 hours.

SECTION 4: BRANDING COMPLIANCE
All marketing materials must be approved by the brand team before publication.
The Waitrose logo must always appear in its approved color format. Font usage
must follow the brand style guide. Unauthorized use of brand assets is prohibited.
